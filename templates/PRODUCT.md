## Non-negotiables
Every agent treats each line as merge-blocking. QA checks all of them on every review.

- Touch targets are at least 48×48 dp with at least 8 dp between them.
- No state is conveyed by colour alone.
- Text sizes are in sp and honour the system font scale.
- Every gesture-driven operation is reachable by a non-gesture path.
